About 7VN Studios

7VN Studios is a creative production studio specializing in event coverage, brand marketing videos, motion design, and video editing for creators.

Whether we're capturing an event and turning it into a compelling visual story, creating marketing videos and motion designs that help brands stand out, or transforming raw footage into polished, publication-ready content, our goal is the same: to make every frame count.

Based in Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria, we work with clients wherever their stories and projects take us.
