About 7VN Studios

7VN Studios covers events, builds marketing videos and motion designs for brands, and edits video for creators. That's the full scope of what we do — no scattered services, just the work we're good at, done properly.

When you bring us an event, we cover it and cut it into something worth watching. When you bring us a brand, we build the marketing video or motion design that gets it noticed. When you bring us raw footage, we edit it into content that's ready to publish.

Based in Ibadan, Nigeria, and working with clients wherever the work takes us.
